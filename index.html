About Carvalho Capital

Born in Timor-Leste.
Built for global markets.

Carvalho Capital Management was founded with the belief that world-class portfolio management shouldn't be limited by geography. Rooted in Timor-Leste, we bring institutional discipline to every investment we make.

We are Timor-Leste's first actively managed equity investment fund, combining rigorous fundamental analysis with systematic risk management. Our mission is to generate consistent, risk-adjusted returns for our investors while building a culture of financial sophistication in Southeast Asia's youngest nation.

Every dollar is invested with conviction, transparency, and discipline. We publish our performance openly, communicate with investors regularly, and never take a position we can't explain.
